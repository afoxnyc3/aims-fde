## Settings

- **Answer model:** `openrouter/anthropic/claude-sonnet-5.5`, provider-default sampling
- **Turn budget:** 6 (agentic techniques); the pipeline makes one call
- **Chunking:** none. One page per record (116 pages, standard + verified tickets); search hits capped at 2000 chars, `read_page` at 12,000
- **Embedding model:** `openrouter/openai/text-embedding-3-small`
- **Embedding tokens (measured):** 30,353 to embed all 116 pages once, from the provider's `usage.total_tokens`; 781 embedded by this run (queries, plus pages if not cached)
- **Hybrid:** RRF, k = 60, over BM25 top 20 and dense top 20
- **Pipeline:** `answer_once`, hybrid top 5, one answer call
- **Scoring:** Session 5's rule, two changes. Pass = every expected term in the answer (underscores read as spaces; a list means any one phrasing) and no decline; a decline question passes on a `CANNOT ANSWER:` line with no forbidden term. Cited = **every** gold page named in the answer (Session 5: any). Substring scoring cannot tell a confident answer from a hedged one; that needs the Week 4 judge.
