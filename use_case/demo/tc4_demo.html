    Every new hire is a free-text ticket someone has to interpret.

    For each one the desk reads the form, works out the role, decides on a system login, then picks the club and level.

    Read this as a gap report

    Four of five erred toward a person. Two are our anonymizer; two are the model missing a club that was on the form, which hid an exception behind it. The fifth is a missing rule in the matrix, not a model error.
