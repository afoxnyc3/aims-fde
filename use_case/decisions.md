## Step 6 — pipeline assessment

For v1 `solve()`, the answers are **no, yes, no, no**:

1. **No:** the steps and branches can be drawn in advance.
2. **Yes:** answering requires retrieval from the published standard, a data source. V1 has no live account lookup; the adapter retrieves JSM input and the caller posts comments outside `solve()`.
3. **No:** validation and ambiguity lead to defined outcomes, not self-directed retries.
4. **No:** ticket language varies, but supported cases and outcomes are bounded.

**Verdict:** 1 yes answer; build a fixed pipeline. Later account creation does not by itself require agency.

**Existing-account boundary:** V1 detects only ambiguity visible in ticket text, such as multiple people or conflicting identifiers. It does not verify account existence, rehire/transfer history, or login-email availability. The associate performs those checks before provisioning; recommendations must not claim those facts are verified or unconditionally direct creation of a new account.

**Strongest counterargument:** Live identity resolution may require searching accounts by one identifier, inspecting a possible match, then choosing another lookup based on that result. An agent could handle that investigation better than fixed branches. Switch only if supported-ticket evidence shows adaptive investigation, rather than missing standards or poor intake, is the pipeline's limitation and an agent improves the result.
